# Validation Report: `agents-office-implementation.plan.md` vs. current repository

## Headline finding (read this first)

The plan's single most load-bearing claim — "Department names and count (8: `exec`, `revenue`, `engineering`, `frontend`, `devops`, `fin`, `success`, `product`) are current and load-bearing throughout" (plan line 12) — **is false**. It describes the *target* department set, not the current one, and the mislabeling propagates into several downstream tasks. Checked directly:

- `src/data.js:17-18`: `export const DEPT_KEYS = ['exec', 'revenue', 'engineering', 'frontend', 'devops', 'secdata', 'fin', 'content'];` with full display names `SECURITY & DATA` (`secdata`) and `CONTENT & COMMS` (`content`) at lines 24 and 26.
- `backend/app/mcp.py:16`: `DEPT_KEYS = ["exec", "revenue", "engineering", "frontend", "devops", "secdata", "fin", "content"]` — hardcoded in the policy layer itself.
- `office.agents.json` (counted directly): departments present are `content`(3), `devops`(5), `secdata`(3), `revenue`(8), `frontend`(4), `exec`(3), `fin`(3), `engineering`(6) = 35 agents, 8 departments.
- `office.config.local.json`'s `mcp.departments` keys (`content`, `secdata`, plus `exec/revenue/engineering/frontend/devops/fin`) match this *exactly*.

There is no `success` or `product` department anywhere in the live code, config, or seed data today. `success` and `product` are the plan's own *target* names for two of the eight slots (replacing `secdata`→Customer Support-shaped `success`, and `content`→Product-shaped `product`) — a functional reassignment, not a rename, and the plan never states this transformation explicitly; it just asserts the target names as "current."

This inverts the framing of **Task 6b** and the "ground truth" section (plan lines 20-24): the plan claims `office.config.local.json`'s `mcp.departments` keys (`content`, `secdata`) are "stale... pre-redesign keys... that don't match the current 8-department roster" and treats fixing this as an urgent, already-broken production misconfiguration gating Task 2. In fact `content`/`secdata` **are** the current department keys — confirmed identically in `office.agents.json`, `src/data.js`, and `mcp.py`'s own `DEPT_KEYS`. Nothing is broken today. The real (and valid) concern buried under this wrong framing is forward-looking: *once* Task 17 actually renames `secdata`→`success`/`content`→`product`, `office.config.local.json` and `mcp.py`'s hardcoded `DEPT_KEYS` constant will both need updating — but that is future work gated on Task 17, not a pre-existing defect Task 6b needs to fix "before or alongside Task 2."

This also breaks **Task 11**'s premise: "pilot department is `success` (5-seat cap)." There is no `success` department to pilot against today, under any name — it doesn't exist until Task 17 creates it. As written, Task 11 cannot start until a renamed/repurposed `success` department exists, which per the plan's own dependency chain only happens at Task 17 — i.e., Task 11 cannot actually precede Task 17 the way the plan's task ordering implies, unless Task 11 is understood to include first standing up a `success` department from scratch (not a "5-seat cap" pilot of something that pre-exists).

A third, unrelated department scheme also exists in the repo: `.claude/AGENTS.md`, `.claude/unused-seats.md`, and `CLAUDE.md`'s routine-department text all describe `emails/sales/marketing/ops/fin/delivery` (6 departments) — an even older scheme than either `secdata/content` or `success/product`. The plan is aware of this drift in one place (the "Application audit" note about `CLAUDE.md` saying "Emails, Accounting and Sales" vs. the live check saying "Content, Finance and Revenue" — correctly flagged, see below) but doesn't connect it to the deeper problem that three incompatible naming generations are live in different files simultaneously.

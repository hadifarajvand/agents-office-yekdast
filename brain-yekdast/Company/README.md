**Readers:** All 12 agents (lexi, piper, mlead, riley, olead, scout, report, dlead, qa, elead, alead, invo) read this before tasks involving customer intake, business decisions, and roadmap planning.

**Purpose:** Company notes, business domain, strategy, and market context.

- `customers.md` — customer profiles, use cases  
- `market.md` — competitive landscape, positioning
- `roadmap.md` — quarterly/annual product roadmap
